Notes

Weather

Summer Garda is hot, then a short thunderstorm. Boats and exposed walls are the days to watch wind. If the lake is angry in the morning, cancel the ferry. Venice in rain is still the train plan. Sigurtà is outdoor; lunch in Valeggio still works.

Etiquette

Buongiorno before you order. Take the shaded table. Do not picnic on Sigurtà flowerbeds. On boats, let people off first; keep the gangway clear. Bardolino is a living town after 18:00: keep voices down on residential streets when the baby is finally asleep.

Tourist traps
  • Sirmione at 11:00 in high season — parking death + ZTL cameras.
  • Gardaland with a six-month-old.
  • Photo menus on Bardolino and Lazise piers; walk one street inland.
  • Monte Baldo cable car queues. Malcesine as a “since we’re north” add-on.
  • Riva in one day with an 18:00 rule. Bergamo Alta with this stroller.
  • Chasing sunset in another town. You live on the lake.
  • Venice: San Marco with a stroller, Burano as a “quick extra.”
Rainy-day plan
  1. Bardolino wine and olive oil morning — already indoor.
  2. Cavaion cellar if you want a short drive.
  3. Valeggio lunch even if Sigurtà is wet.
  4. Desenzano or Mantua cafes. Verona if you wanted a city anyway.
  5. Venice train still wins versus lagoon traffic.
Emergency

Italy single emergency number 112. Ambulance 118. Police 113. Fire 115. Roadside ACI 803.116.

Bardolino seasonal tourist medical studio: Via Croce 35, typically morning hours in season, tel +39 045 511 7746. Confirm current dates at the comune. Farmacie: green cross; after hours the door lists the farmacia di turno.

East-shore hospital ER pointer: Ospedale Pederzoli, Via Monte Baldo 24, Peschiera del Garda. Tourist office: Piazzale Aldo Moro 5, Bardolino, tel +39 045 721 0078.

A six-day shape (you still choose)
  1. Day 1 — Cisano / Bardolino beaches. Unpack. Learn the lungolago.
  2. Day 2 — Peschiera fortress and lido, or Lazise if you want shorter.
  3. Day 3 — Sigurtà and Borghetto (or Cavaion if the sky looks wet).
  4. Day 4 — Wine and olive oil morning. Laundry. Nap.
  5. Day 5 — Venice on a weekday, or Mantua / Verona if you skip the lagoon.
  6. Day 6 — Ferry if the lake is kind; Garda town or Soave if it is not.
